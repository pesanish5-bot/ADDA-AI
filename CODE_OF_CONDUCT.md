# Code of conduct

We want ADDA AI to be a welcoming place for hackathon teammates and outside contributors. Be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and make room for people with different experience levels. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful comments, close threads, or block participation when needed to keep the project safe. If you encounter a problem, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Do not post personal details or security-sensitive information in a public issue.
